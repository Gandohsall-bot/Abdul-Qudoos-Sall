Personal Narrative

Building Sustainable Solutions Grounded in Technical Integrity.

"Engineering is not simply about pouring concrete or assembling steel beams; it is the discipline of solving physical challenges with absolute safety, economic balance, and lasting societal value."

I earned my Bachelor of Engineering (B.Eng.) in Civil Engineering from the historic Fourah Bay College, University of Sierra Leone. Throughout my academic career, I focused extensively on structural analysis, fluid mechanics, foundation design, and construction materials technology.

Transitioning from theoretical models to applied engineering, I have gained hands-on experience through civil engineering internships and engineering assistant roles. My responsibilities have encompassed developing precision AutoCAD drawings, modeling framing schemes with ETABS and STAAD.Pro, performing quantity take-offs, and conducting physical site observations on active building and roadway projects.

Whether collaborating with senior project directors, consulting with geotechnical specialists, or detailing bar bending schedules for on-site tradesmen, I prioritize clear technical communication, code compliance (BS 8110 / Eurocode 2), and proactive problem mitigation.

Professional Mission

To deliver dependable civil engineering design and project management services that protect public safety, optimize construction expenditure, and promote resilient infrastructure across developing urban environments.

Guiding Philosophy

Core Engineering Principles

Four foundational standards that steer every structural calculation, site assessment, and drafting layout.

Safety & Structural Integrity

Safety is non-negotiable. Every beam, column, and foundation must be designed with uncompromising factor-of-safety margins, strict code compliance, and verified load paths.

Constructability on Site

A design is only as good as its real-world execution. Drawings must anticipate field constraints, avoiding congested rebar nodes and ensuring materials are locally attainable.

Climate & Hydrological Resilience

Engineering for tropical climates requires robust stormwater drainage, effective slope stabilization, and high-durability concrete mixtures resistant to weathering.

Transparent Communication

Accurate reporting, transparent change tracking, and disciplined documentation prevent costly site discrepancies and protect the project timeline.
