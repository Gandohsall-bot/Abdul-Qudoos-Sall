Applying analytical rigor, standardized design codes, and advanced engineering software across 6 key civil engineering sectors from initial assessment through structural delivery.

Discipline 01

Structural Engineering & Reinforced Concrete

Eurocode 2 / BS 8110 Finite Element Modeling

Specializing in the structural analysis and detailing of reinforced concrete (RC) frames, shear walls, continuous beams, one-way/two-way slabs, and structural steel connections. Every design is developed to verify ultimate limit states (bending, shear, axial capacity) and serviceability limit states (deflection, crack width control).

Core Capabilities

  • Dead, live, wind, and seismic lateral load calculation
  • Multi-storey moment frame analysis in ETABS & SAP2000
  • Reinforcement area calculation and rebar anchorage detailing
  • Deep foundation & mat foundation load transfer

Standard Deliverables

  • Structural General Arrangement (GA) drawings
  • Detailed beam, column, and foundation reinforcement sheets
  • Bar bending schedules (BBS) to BS 8666
  • Structural calculation notes for municipal building permit submissions

Primary Software

AutoCAD ETABS STAAD.Pro Revit SAP2000

Discipline 03

Transportation & Highway Infrastructure

AASHTO Standards Geometric Alignment

Designing and evaluating municipal roadways, commuter corridors, pavement rehabilitation programs, and associated right-of-way infrastructure to foster safe, resilient urban mobility.

Design Scope

  • Horizontal and vertical centerline profile alignments
  • Flexible pavement design (asphalt concrete, base course, subgrade CBR)

Deliverables

  • Earthwork cut-and-fill cross-section balances
  • Pavement distress surveys and rehabilitation specifications
  • Drainage inlet and culvert junction drawings

Discipline 04

Geotechnical Engineering & Foundation Design

Soil Mechanics Slope Stability

Analyzing subterranean soil mechanics, shear strength parameters, consolidation characteristics, and bearing capacities to design stable foundations and reinforced slope retention systems.

Technical Scope

  • Allowable bearing capacity calculation (Terzaghi & Meyerhof formulas)
  • Borehole log interpretation and soil classification (USCS)
  • Slope slip-circle factor of safety (Bishop's & Spencer methods)
  • Earth retaining wall sizing (cantilever RC, gravity, gabion)

Deliverables

  • Geotechnical borehole cross-section profiles
  • Shallow footing and deep pile layout drawings
  • Cantilever retaining wall reinforcement details with weep holes
  • Slope stabilization mitigation drawings

Tools & Analysis

GEO5 AutoCAD Soil Lab Testing Protocols Settlement Analysis

Discipline 05

Water Resources & Stormwater Drainage

Hydraulic Modeling Flood Mitigation

Designing hydraulic channels, storm sewers, retention basins, and culverts capable of managing intense tropical stormwater discharges to safeguard road networks and residential settlements from flooding.

Hydraulic Analysis

  • Catchment peak discharge estimation (Rational Method Q = CIA)
  • Open channel Manning's equation velocity and bed-slope sizing
  • Box culvert and pipe culvert inlet/outlet control sizing
  • Sediment trap and energy dissipator design

Deliverables

  • Stormwater drainage master layout drawings
  • Longitudinal drain channel gradient profiles
  • Precast & cast-in-situ culvert structural drawings
  • Hydrological calculation documentation

Tools

Autodesk Civil 3D EPA SWMM AutoCAD Hydraulic Worksheets
